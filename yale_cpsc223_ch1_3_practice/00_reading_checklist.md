# Reading Checklist — Yale Notes Through Chapter 3

Use this checklist while reading. Do not copy notes word-for-word. Your goal is to produce commands, files, and debugging evidence.

## Chapter 1 — Course administration / overview

Focus on:

- What the course is trying to teach
- Why C is useful
- Why data structures matter
- What outside resources/documentation are expected

Deliverable:

- In `01_environment_check/environment.md`, write 3–5 sentences explaining why learning C and Linux tooling helps your C++/systems path.

## Chapter 2 — The Zoo / developing environment

Deliverable:

## Chapter 3 — Linux programming environment

Focus on:

- Shell commands: `pwd`, `ls`, `cd`, `mkdir`, `cp`, `mv`, `rm`, `cat`, `less`, `grep`, `wc`, `sort`, `uniq`, `diff`
- Filesystem paths: absolute vs relative
- Redirection: `>`, `>>`, `<`, `2>`, `|`
- Editors: use Neovim/Vim if that is your setup
- `gcc` warnings and debug builds
- `make` and Makefiles
- Assertions
- `gdb` basics: breakpoints, run, next, step, print, backtrace, continue
- Valgrind basics
- Git basics: init, status, add, commit, log, diff

Deliverables:

- Complete `02_shell_redirection/`
- Complete `03_gcc_make/`
- Complete `04_debugging_gdb_valgrind/`
- Complete `05_git_lab/`
